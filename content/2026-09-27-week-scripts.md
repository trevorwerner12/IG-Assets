## Filming setup (applies to every day)

- **Phone:** vertical 4K at 30 fps. For pours and drops, use Slo-mo (120 fps).
- **Light:** face a window, or put a lamp in front of you and off to one side. No overhead kitchen light alone.
- **Two angles:** (1) tripod at chest height across the counter, 1x lens; (2) overhead, phone clamped or held directly above the bowl, 0.5x or 1x.
- **Text:** keep on-screen text in the middle third of the screen. The bottom 20% and the right edge are covered by Instagram's buttons and caption.
- **Edit:** cut every 1–2 seconds. The first frame is the hook, so never open on a blank counter.
- **Signature opener:** 1 second of deadpan stare into the lens, holding the food. Use it on Sun, Tue, and Sat so it becomes recognizable.
